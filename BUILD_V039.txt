Speedline IPTV Portal v0.3.9

- Fixes recently viewed live channels failing to reopen when the user switches
  back before the 90-second resolved-link cache expires.
- Invalidates a live channel's cached Stalker create_link as soon as playback
  leaves that channel, so returning always resolves a fresh playable session.
- Direct Chameleon/Astra HTTP URLs are not cached by this mechanism and remain
  unchanged; the fix only rebuilds their player connection.
- Retains the v0.3.8 five-second frozen-playback recovery, low-latency Media3
  buffers, three-second first-frame check, TextureView output, channel-list UI,
  and movie remote controls.

Speedline IPTV Portal v0.3.8

- Detects a live channel whose playback position has stopped advancing for about
  5 seconds after video has started.
- Automatically rebuilds the player connection for the same channel; no manual
  channel change is required. If the portal supplied a temporary create_link URL,
  its cached value is invalidated before reconnecting. Direct Chameleon/Astra URLs
  are left unchanged and are simply reopened.
- Uses a smaller Media3 live-stream buffer (2-10 seconds), starts with 350 ms of
  buffered media, and resumes after rebuffering with 750 ms to reduce channel-open
  delay while retaining protection against short network jitter.
- Limits video joining time to 500 ms and keeps decoder fallback enabled.
- Retains the v0.3.7 TextureView black-screen fix, 3-second first-frame check,
  channel-list overlay, and film seek/play controls.
